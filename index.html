Research and Industry Projects

1. Optimal Portfolio Selection Using Mixture Designs

Application of statistical mixture-design methodologies to financial portfolio selection, with an emphasis on identifying portfolios with desirable volatility characteristics.

2. Leveraged ETF Performance Evaluation

Empirical analysis of leveraged exchange-traded funds using daily, weekly, and monthly returns. The analysis examines leverage ratios, regression, volatility, correlation, and distributional characteristics.

3. Financial Data Analysis

Statistical exploration and modeling of financial data using descriptive statistics, regression techniques, graphical analysis, and computational tools.
