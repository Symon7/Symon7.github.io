About Me

I am a PhD candidate in Applied Mathematical Sciences, specializing in Applied Statistics at Oakland University, Michigan. My academic background combines advanced training in statistics and mathematics with extensive university teaching experience.

My research focuses on statistical methodologies for financial data analysis, optimal portfolio selection, mixture-design experiments, and performance evaluation of leveraged exchange-traded funds. I am interested in applying statistical modeling and Computational methods to practical financial and data science problems.

Alongside my research, I bring more than 14 years of university teaching experience in mathematics and statistics-related education.
