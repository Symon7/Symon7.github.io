Welcome to my homepage!

I am a PhD candidate in Applied Mathematical Sciences (Applied Statistics) at Oakland University, Michigan, USA. I expect to complete my doctoral degree in June 2027.

My research interests include statistical finance, optimal portfolio selection, financial data analytics, mixture-design experiments, and performance evaluation of leveraged exchange-traded funds.

I have more than 14 years of university teaching experience in mathematics, along with graduate training in applied statistics and mathematics.

This website presents my academic research, publications, teaching experience, and applied work in financial analytics and data science.
